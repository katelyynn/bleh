# Music
- __Integrated LRCLIB for much better Lyrics support__
	- Lyrics are now requested by bleh super fast and displayed in a small preview on the right sidebar of track pages. Click this snippet to open a full dialog containing all lyrics, clicking a lyric copies it to your clipboard and scrolls down automatically. You can search for a specific lyric and toggle romanisation for japanese/korean scripts.
	- Additionally, you can right-click any track and hit *View lyrics* to open this same dialog from anywhere on the site.
	- The native Last.fm lyrics are now hidden as they became redundant. They’re still technically accessible if you append /+lyrics to a URL though.
- Refreshed the look of track videos
- Updated the *Albums* section to half use TSX
	- This fixes the header being broken, along with refreshing the design of it a little bit. Still pending a full rewrite to be component-based.
- Migrated some of the campfire *Home* page to use TSX
	- This is pending a rewrite too to support trackpad scrolling better etc.
- Fixed empty music links causing an error (I think)

- Right-click menus now exist for your Top track/obsession and top-right Music status (Pro only)
